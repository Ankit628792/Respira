# Respira - User Guide & Feature Manual 📖

Welcome to **Respira**, your personal breath training and lung wellness companion. This guide explains how to use all features of the application to improve your breathing habits and respiratory health.

---

## 🫁 1. Dashboard (Home Screen)

Upon opening Respira, the Home Screen provides a consolidated view of your daily training state:

* **Daily Progress Bar**: Displays total mindful breathing minutes logged each day over the past 7 days.
* **Vital Capacity Summary**: Shows your latest estimated lung volume ($L$) and performance rating. If no tests have been completed yet, an empty state banner invites you to take your first diagnostic.
* **Recommended Session**: Quick-start card for the recommended breathing routine based on your activity patterns.
* **Offline Indicator**: A subtle status badge confirming that all your metrics and records remain 100% offline and stored securely on your device.

---

## 🧘 2. Guided Breathing Sessions

1. **Select an Exercise**:
   * Navigate to the **Exercises** tab from the bottom navigation bar.
   * Choose from 4 core routines:
     * **Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)**: Calms anxiety and increases focus.
     * **4-7-8 Relaxing Breath**: Deep nervous system relaxation for sleep.
     * **Diaphragmatic Deep Breathing**: Strengthens the diaphragm and boosts oxygen intake.
     * **Awakening Energizing Breath**: Rapid oxygenation to boost energy.

2. **Start the Session**:
   * Tap **Start Session**.
   * An interactive breathing visualizer dynamically expands during **Inhale**, holds steady during **Hold**, contracts during **Exhale**, and pauses during **Hold Out**.
   * Synchronized audio cues guide your pace without needing to watch the screen continuously.

3. **Complete & Log**:
   * Upon completion, a session summary popup displays your total duration, completed cycles, and streak update. You can add custom notes before saving.

---

## ⏱ 3. Breath-Hold Lung Capacity Test

1. Navigate to the **Test** tab from the bottom navigation bar.
2. Read the safety instructions: Sit comfortably and relax before starting.
3. Tap **Begin Diagnostic**.
4. **Phase 1 - Deep Preparation Breath**: Follow the 5-second countdown to take a full deep inhalation.
5. **Phase 2 - Maximum Breath Hold**: Hold your breath while maintaining the touch button (or tapping **Release when Done**).
6. **Phase 3 - Exhale & Diagnostic Results**: Release and view your calculated metrics:
   * Estimated Vital Capacity ($L$)
   * Estimated Lung Age
   * FEV1 (Forced Expiratory Volume)
   * Peak Expiratory Flow (PEF)
7. Save the test record to track your long-term trend curve.

---

## 📊 4. History & Analytics

* Navigate to the **History** tab.
* Filter your records by **All**, **Sessions**, or **Lung Tests**.
* Inspect exact timestamps, durations, completed cycles, and capacity notes.
* If no history exists yet, a clean placeholder indicates that you can start logging sessions to build your history.

---

## 👤 5. Profile & Settings

* **Profile**: View lifetime statistics (streak count, total sessions, total mindful minutes) and unlockable wellness milestones.
* **Settings**:
  * Customize breathing visualizer animation style (*Pulsing Circle*, *Glowing Lungs*, *Expanding Waves*).
  * Toggle audio cues and haptic vibration feedback.
  * Reset local history & statistics whenever needed.
